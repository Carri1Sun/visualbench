# 06-device-rise — Device Rise

Create a six-second product reveal. Draw a premium smartphone with code on a pale background. It rises from below the frame, starts tilted in perspective and settles upright with a gentle deceleration. After settling, show three staggered notifications: "Your trip is ready", "3 places saved", "Share with Maya". The device occupies about two-thirds of the frame height.

1920 × 1080 · 30 fps · 6 s · 180 frames
