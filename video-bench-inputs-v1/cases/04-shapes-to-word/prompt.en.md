# 04-shapes-to-word — Shapes To Word

Make a 10-second ident for WAYPOINT. Eight colorful filled geometric shapes become the eight letters of WAYPOINT through a visible shape transformation. Use the supplied logo as a separate mark. In the last section the mark travels left to right and removes each letter only as it passes that letter. Keep the word readable before the exit.

1920 × 1080 · 30 fps · 10 s · 300 frames
