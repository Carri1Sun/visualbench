# 15-beat-montage — Beat Montage

Create a 20-second travel-brand montage using music.wav, the supplied vector postcards, and the three MP4 clips. The assets are original stylized illustrations, not photographic footage. Let the music dictate major cuts and the build toward the final brand card. Use at least four postcards and two clips. Keep on-screen copy short and end on the provided brand line.

1920 × 1080 · 30 fps · 20 s · 600 frames
