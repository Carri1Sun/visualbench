# 11-speaker-captions — Speaker Captions

Use source.mp4 and transcript.json to create an 11.5-second captioned clip. Keep the speaker performance and original audio timing. Replace the green background with a designed simple background. Use the supplied foreground mask to place at least one large keyword BEHIND the person, with normal captions in front. Choose emphasis from the actual transcript. This is a green-screen compositing variant, not a natural-background segmentation test.

1280 × 720 · 30 fps · 11.5 s · 345 frames
