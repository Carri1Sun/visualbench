# 12-news-highlight — News Highlight

Use article.png unchanged as the source image. Build an 8-second editorial insert on a white background: initial blur resolves, the article moves slightly toward the camera with subtle 3D rotation, and a rough marker sweeps behind the exact phrases in highlights.json. Infer their locations from the screenshot or OCR; the marker must not cover the letter strokes.

1920 × 1080 · 30 fps · 8 s · 240 frames

## Supplied files (relative to public/input/)

- `article.png`
- `fonts/DejaVuSans.ttf`
- `fonts/LICENSE.txt`
- `highlights.json`
