# 14-audio-spectrum — Audio Spectrum

Analyze music.wav and render a 16-second spectrum with 32 frequency bars. The bar values must derive from the actual sound, not random or purely periodic animation. Use a magenta-to-cyan palette with restrained glow and reflection. Make the low-only, high-only and silent regions perceptibly different. Parameters for bar count, color and glow should be easy to edit.

1920 × 1080 · 30 fps · 16 s · 480 frames

## Supplied files (relative to public/input/)

- `music.json`
- `music.wav`
