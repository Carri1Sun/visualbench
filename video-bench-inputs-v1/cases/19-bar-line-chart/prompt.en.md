# 19-bar-line-chart — Bar Line Chart

Use monthly.json. Make a 12-second chart sequence: revenue bars grow from the baseline month by month; the conversion-rate line is then drawn over them with a moving tip marker. Give dollars and percent separate labeled scales. Finish by emphasizing June and the change from January. Do not distort scale to exaggerate the result.
