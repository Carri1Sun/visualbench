# 16-travel-route — Travel Route

Make a 20-second map travel story: Los Angeles to New York to Paris. Use cities.json and world-map.svg. Keep map projection and coordinates consistent, draw the route progressively, and move the camera along it. At Paris reveal a dimensional Eiffel Tower built with code. The provided map is a deliberately simplified geographic diagram, not a navigation map.

1920 × 1080 · 30 fps · 20 s · 600 frames

## Supplied files (relative to public/input/)

- `cities.json`
- `world-map.svg`
