- Task 11 source.mp4: https://remotion.media/greenscreen.mp4 . Distribution statement: https://remotion.media/ . The catalog describes its fixtures as freely usable for testing, royalty-free and without attribution. The file is included under that upstream statement; no new license or ownership is asserted.
