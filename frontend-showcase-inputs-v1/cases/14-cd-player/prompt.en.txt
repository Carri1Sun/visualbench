Build a low-poly, PS1-inspired 3D CD player experience using albums.json and the supplied original cover SVGs. Clicking an album reveals its disc and a Play action. Playing should insert that disc into the player, move its cover aside and visibly reorganize the remaining stack. Only one disc can be active. Choose the machine, camera, lighting and UI design. Audio failure must not break the visual interaction.
