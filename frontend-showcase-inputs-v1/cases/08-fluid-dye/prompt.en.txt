Build a two-dimensional interactive dye-flow simulation. Pointer dragging injects color and momentum; dye should be transported through a persistent velocity field, with visible swirling and gradual dissipation. Choose a numerical method suitable for a compact browser demo. A pressure-solving fluid method is preferred, but a disclosed approximation is acceptable; do not describe ordinary random particles as a physical fluid. Include Clear, Pause/Resume and three palette presets. Start with a small automatic dye injection so the first frame is not blank. Choose the UI and visual treatment yourself.
